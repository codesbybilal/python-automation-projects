Brightwave Software Solutions reported strong performance in the third quarter of 2025, achieving total revenue of $4.8 million and a net profit of $620,000. Growth was supported by the launch of a new subscription analytics product and decreased cloud infrastructure costs, while active customer accounts increased to 171 and churn declined to 2.1%.

Subscription revenue reached $2.9 million, custom development projects contributed $1.4 million, and support and maintenance added $500,000. Operating expenses totaled $3.9 million with an increased headcount of 44 employees, and sales and marketing investments facilitated an expansion into the European market with new enterprise customers in Germany and the Netherlands.

Management identified key risks including client concentration, engineering salary pressure, and data-protection compliance requirements, while outlining mitigation strategies such as customer diversification. Looking ahead to Q4, the company projects revenue between $5.2 and $5.5 million and aims to launch a mobile companion app, hire a compliance officer, and open an Amsterdam sales office.
