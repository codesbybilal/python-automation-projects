This Master Services Agreement is established between Harborview Legal Partners LLP and Crestline Logistics Inc. with an effective date of March 1, 2025. The Firm will provide corporate legal advisory services such as contract review, regulatory compliance guidance, and commercial dispute representation as the Client's primary outside counsel.

The agreement outlines fee structures based on hourly rates for partners, senior associates, and paralegals, along with monthly invoicing, late payment interest, and expense reimbursement terms. Confidentiality obligations are mandated for both parties, lasting for five years post-termination, and details are provided for permitted disclosures required by law or court order.

The initial term of the agreement is two years with automatic one-year renewals, and provisions are included for termination, liability limitations, New York governing law, and a mandatory dispute escalation process prior to binding arbitration.
